Like the trumpets used in the battle of Jericho (Josh. 6), these trumpets lead up to the fall of the worldly city (11:8 note, 11:13), and in the seventh trumpet the complete victory of God arrives. The trumpet plagues are esp. modeled on the plagues against Egypt, signifying God’s judgments on idolatrous power.\n\nThe seven seals began with announcements of riders commissioned to bring calamities (6:1–8 note).
