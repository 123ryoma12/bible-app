In particular, chapters 1–3 do not consistently employ line parallelism, which is a common mark of Hebrew poetry. On the other hand, this material is not normal Hebrew prose either. Although it employs certain markers of narrative, such as the sign of the direct object, the relative pronoun, and the Hebrew article, it is not consistent in using those features.
