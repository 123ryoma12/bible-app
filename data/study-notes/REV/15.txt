Like the trumpets, the bowls are modeled on the plagues against Egypt. But the bowls may result in more severe judgments than did the trumpets. The trumpet judgments typically affected a third of the total, but the bowls affect the whole.\n\nThese bowls symbolize the judgments of God against evildoers.
