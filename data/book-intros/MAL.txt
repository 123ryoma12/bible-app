The latter translates the phrase “by Malachi” as “by the hand of his angel [or ‘messenger’].” The main arguments against viewing Malachi as the personal name of the prophet are the absence of specific data concerning his father and the lack of any mention of his place of birth. Nevertheless, these are not sufficient grounds for rejecting Malachi as the personal name of an individual prophet. In the case of every other Old Testament prophetic book, a personal name is given. God promised to send a “messenger of the covenant” who would refine the people, no one less than the Lord Jesus Christ (1–3).
