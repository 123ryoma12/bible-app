The wisdom of Proverbs is the fruit of experience and reflection, stressing the need to think through and apply human knowledge and the Word of God to all affairs of life in a responsible way.\n\nAs is sometimes the case in the Bible, the chapter divisions are rather arbitrary and do not aid the analysis of the text. Each sentence has its own social setting and special aim.
