The closest parallels are the love poems found in the Song of Solomon. This song is appropriate for a royal wedding and was probably used in many royal weddings throughout the history of Israel. The psalm has four strophes (Introduction to Hebrew Poetry): the introduction (v. 1), the praise of the king (vv. 2–9), the calling of the queen (vv. 10–15), and the conclusion (vv. 16, 17). Since the Davidic kingship reflects God’s ultimate kingship and anticipates Christ as king, the psalm has a second application to Christ, the king and bridegroom, and His bride, the church (Eph. 5:25–32).
