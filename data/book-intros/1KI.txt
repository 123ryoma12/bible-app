So, for example, we read, “Now in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam the son of Nebat, Abijam began to reign over Judah” (1 Kin. 15:1). This is sometimes followed by the age of the king when he came to the throne, such as, “Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign” (1 Kin. 22:42). The length of the king’s reign is then included. His ancestry appears next: for the Judean kings the mother’s name is usually provided, and for the Israelite kings it is normally the father’s name. Finally, the introductory section provides a theological summary of the nature of the king’s reign.
