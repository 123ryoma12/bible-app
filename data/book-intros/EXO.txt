The third theme issues from the first two and is their consummation: the reestablishment of God’s dwelling with people. Each of these themes involves a triumph of divine grace: God’s mighty rescue of His people from slavery in Egypt, His thunderous self-revelation at Sinai, and His gracious condescension to dwell among His erring people in the tabernacle.
