However, there is some question as to whether the prophet’s ministry preceded or followed Josiah’s great reform of the nation’s worship in 622 B.C. Zephaniah’s denunciation of continuing syncretistic worship (mixing the idolatry of the surrounding peoples with the worship of the Lord) and of Baal worship may point to a date before Josiah’s reforms, or it may point to their partial success. Since only a remnant of Baal remained (1:4), Zephaniah probably began to minister after Josiah’s initial reforms in 628 B.C. (2 Chr. 34:3 note). Certainly Nineveh had not yet been overthrown (2:13–15); therefore, the prophet’s message was spoken before its destruction in 612 B.C. Zephaniah would have been a contemporary of Jeremiah, whose call came in Josiah’s thirteenth year (627 B.C.).
