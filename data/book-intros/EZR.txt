The narrative makes clear that past generations of returnees had to struggle against opposition as they attempted to live lives pleasing to God. They would have been unable to achieve what they did without divine assistance, sought through prayer. Furthermore, the community was called on to organize around the worship of God in the temple and in obedience to his covenant requirements. By separating from inappropriate interaction with those around them, the community would avoid jeopardizing its witness. The way forward for the original readers of the history was to emulate past prayerful dependence on God and adherence to His requirements.
