In 1:13, Paul exhorts Timothy “follow the pattern of sound words” that Timothy had heard from Paul (cf. 2:2). This is the origin of the notion of orthodoxy, a “straight teaching” by which all teaching and views must be judged. Over the centuries, church traditions were added to the depositum fide and regarded as part of the deposit of faith itself even when they could not be reasonably traced back to the actual writings of the apostles. This led Martin Luther to declare in one of his Ninety-Five Theses, “The church’s true treasure is the gospel of Jesus Christ.” The gospel is the deposit entrusted to the apostles and handed down from one generation to another via the faithful teaching and preaching of the apostolic witness, namely, sacred Scripture.
