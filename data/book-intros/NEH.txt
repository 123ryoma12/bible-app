It is a mixture of narrative and source material (lists and genealogies). However, the story line in Nehemiah is stronger, partly because of the shorter length of time covered (about thirteen years) and partly because a greater part of the book is a first-person account of Nehemiah’s activities as civil leader in Jerusalem (chs. 1–7; 12:27–13:31). However, all Nehemiah’s initial reforms were thrown into question, and in large measure they had to be repeated (ch. 13).
