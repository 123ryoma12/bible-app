On the other hand, the tabernacle provides a way of approach to the Lord. As the author of Hebrews makes clear, the tabernacle and its ministry were insufficient in themselves (Heb. 10:1–4). Rather, they foreshadowed the final and completely sufficient priestly work of Christ (Heb. 10:11–18). The tabernacle was also viewed as a model of the cosmos, anticipating a future time when God’s presence would fill the whole earth.
