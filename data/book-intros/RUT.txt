While this verb can simply indicate one’s physical return to a particular place, it is often used in Scripture to refer to spiritual renewal (e.g., Hos. 3:5; 6:1; 7:10). Having lost all of the males in her life, which means losing all forms of temporal security and provision, Naomi begins to be filled as Boaz, through Ruth, provides her with grain (2:18; 3:17), and she experiences poetic fullness when Ruth and Boaz provide her with a son (4:13–17).","2) Cost of disobedience.
